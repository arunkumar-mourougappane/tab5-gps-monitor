What constrains this

Before you pick

Static decoration is free

radarBg is a sprite rendered once at boot and blitted whole each frame — it also erases the previous frame's dots. Ticks, bezels and labels baked into it cost sprite RAM and boot time, and nothing per frame. Anything that must move with the heading cannot live there.

A bezel costs radius

The sprite is centred at y=299 inside a card interior spanning 116–482, so it cannot exceed 366 px square. It is 350 today: radius 159 plus 16 px of label padding. Every pixel of tick ring comes out of the circle.

Heading only exists while moving

The needle is course-over-ground from the GPS, which is meaningless below about 1 km/h — that is why it currently vanishes when parked. A heading that survives standing still needs a magnetometer; worth confirming whether the Tab5's IMU has one before designing around it.

North-up is the convention

Satellite azimuths in GSV are north-referenced. Rotating the plot means every reader has to re-map them, and it breaks the pre-rendered sprite — which is why option four is shown but not recommended.
